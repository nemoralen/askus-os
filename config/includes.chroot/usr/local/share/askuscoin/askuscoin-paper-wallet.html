How to use it safely
  1. Open this page offline (ASKus OS offline mode is ideal). Generate, print, then click Clear screen and close the page.
  2. Send ASK to the RECEIVE address. You can check the balance any time without exposing the key.
  3. To spend: in Electrum ASKus choose Wallet → Private keys → Sweep and scan the private key QR. Sweep moves all coins into your Electrum wallet — then treat that paper as used.
  4. Or import into the node: askuscoin-cli importdescriptors '[{"desc":"wpkh(WIF)#checksum","timestamp":"now"}]'
  5. Make two copies, store separately, and never photograph the private half.
